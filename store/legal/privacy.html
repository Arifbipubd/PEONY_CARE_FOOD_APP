UDUFood Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

UDUFood / Peony Care (“we”, “us”) helps Singapore residents find complementary meals from restaurants. This policy covers receivers and restaurant partners.

Receivers · Restaurant partners

Receivers

What we collect

  • Account: your first name and Singapore mobile number.
  • Location: only when you enable GPS to find food near you.
  • Activity: claims, pickup history, and no-show count.

How we use it

We use your data to show food donations near you, send SMS verification codes, and remind you about pickups you've claimed. We don't sell your data - ever.

PDPA compliance

Peony Care complies with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). You can request to view, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing support@udufood.com.

Sharing

Restaurants only see your claim code and first name at pickup. They never see your phone number, location, or claim history. Donors never see any of your details.

How long we keep it

Your data is retained while your account is open. After deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days. Anonymised claim statistics may be retained for impact reporting.

Service providers

  • Twilio - SMS delivery for OTP codes.
  • Cloudflare - security and DDoS protection.
  • Iconify - icon assets (no personal data).

Where your data lives

All personal data is stored in Singapore on AWS ap-southeast-1. It does not leave Singapore except for transient transmission to the service providers listed above.

Age requirement

Peony Care is for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has signed up, email support@udufood.com and we'll remove the account.

Restaurant partners

What we collect

  • Business: restaurant name, UEN, address, opening hours.
  • Contact: owner/manager name, business email, business mobile.
  • Operations: donations posted, claims received, sponsored orders, payout history.

How we use it

We use your data to list your restaurant on the platform for receivers to find, manage claims and sponsored orders, and pay you for sponsored donations. We don't sell your data - ever.

PDPA compliance

Peony Care complies with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). You can request to view, correct, or delete your business profile at any time by emailing support@udufood.com.

What's public vs private

  • Public: your restaurant name, address, hours, and dish photos - visible to all receivers and donors.
  • Private: your contact email, contact mobile, UEN, and payout records - never shown to other users.

How long we keep it

Business data is retained while your restaurant account is open. After deletion: contact details removed within 30 days; UEN and tax-related records retained 7 years per ACRA and IRAS requirements; payout records retained 7 years.

Service providers

  • Twilio - SMS delivery for OTP codes.
  • Stripe - payout processing for sponsored donations.
  • Cloudflare - security and DDoS protection.
  • Iconify - icon assets (no personal data).

Where your data lives

Business data is stored in Singapore on AWS ap-southeast-1. Payment data is processed by Stripe (Singapore region).

Age requirement

Restaurant accounts must be opened by an authorised representative aged 18 or above. We rely on ACRA UEN verification to confirm business authenticity.

Questions about your data? Email support@udufood.com.
