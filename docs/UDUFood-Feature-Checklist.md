# UDUFood — feature checklist (one-pager)

For the marketing team. Walk a restaurant owner through **1 → 10**. Tick each item as you cover it.

**Language:** always **complementary meal** (never “free meal”). Always **UDUFood**. Skip **Donor** for now — do not mention sponsors or payments.

**Radius (correction):** receivers do **not** need to be 500 m away. By default they see listings **within 5 km**. In Location settings they can change that to **1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 10 km**. Pickup is at the restaurant — they come in and scan the QR.

---

- [ ] **1. Phone login & two roles**  
  Login is phone number + OTP only. Two roles: **Restaurant** (posts leftover food) and **Receiver** (claims one complementary meal a day). Email is for invoices, not login.

- [ ] **2. Restaurant signup — instant access**  
  Restaurant registers with business details and **UEN**. There is **no approval wait**. Once registered, they go straight into the restaurant portal and can post immediately.

- [ ] **3. Post a complementary meal**  
  From Home, tap **Post**. Fill in dish name, category (rice, noodles, cooked meal, bakery, etc.), quantity (packs / portions / boxes…), optional photo, notes, and schedule: **one-time**, **every day**, or **custom days**. No fee to post — ever.

- [ ] **4. QR code at the counter**  
  After posting, the app creates a **QR** for that listing. Print it or show it on the phone. The receiver scans the **food QR** in-store. That scan is the pickup — it is **automatically marked Collected**. The restaurant does **not** confirm or tap anything.

- [ ] **5. How receivers find the restaurant (5 km default)**  
  Receivers browse meals and restaurants **near them**. Default search radius is **5 km**. They can change it in settings to **1, 2, 3, 5, or 10 km** — whatever they pick is what they see. Distance is shown on each listing (e.g. 1.2 km).

- [ ] **6. Search, filters & restaurant page**  
  Receivers can search by name, switch **Meals / Restaurants**, and filter by category, distance. Tapping a restaurant opens its public page (menu photos, address, current listings).

- [ ] **7. Instant claim — one per day**  
  Receiver opens the listing, comes to the shop, scans the **food-specific QR**. The meal is **claimed and marked Collected automatically**. Limit is **1 complementary meal per receiver per Singapore calendar day** (resets at midnight SGT). If the food is gone, they see unavailable. If they already claimed today, they see the daily-limit screen.

- [ ] **8. Today’s claims board**  
  Restaurant opens **Claims** to see today’s pickups. Scanning the food QR already marks them **Collected** — staff do not mark collected by hand. If someone doesn’t show, mark **No-show** — that portion goes back so someone else can claim it.

- [ ] **9. Manage listings**  
  **Manage** has Active / Past / Inactive. Close a listing anytime (run out or stop). Reactivate later. Edit details. Recurring posts (every day / custom days) so they don’t retype a regular leftover.

- [ ] **10. Dashboard, profile & extras**  
  **Home** shows today’s numbers (posted, claimed, remaining). **Menu photos** for the public page. **Notifications** when something is claimed. **Analytics** (what moved, claim rate). **Profile** to edit details, location, help. Posting is **free for restaurant partners**.
