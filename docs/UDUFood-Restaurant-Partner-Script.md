# UDUFood — Restaurant partner script

How to explain the app to a restaurant owner · Singapore  
Internal use

Use this when you visit a kitchen, stall, or cafe. Read the 60-second opener first. If they have two minutes, walk the full pitch, then show the phone.

**Always say complementary meal — never “free meal.” Always say UDUFood.**

---

## 01 — 60-second opener (door / phone)

> Hi, I’m from UDUFood. We help Singapore restaurants turn leftover food into complementary meals for people nearby who need them.
>
> You post what you have — leftover rice, a few portions, end-of-day dishes. People nearby — by default within 5 km, or whatever radius they set in the app — can find your listing and claim one complementary meal a day by scanning a QR code in the shop. You don’t take orders, you don’t process payment, and there is no fee to join or to post.
>
> You stay in control: you choose the dish, the quantity, and you can close the listing anytime. If someone doesn’t show up, you tap No-show and that portion goes back so someone else can claim it.
>
> Can I show you how it looks on the phone? It takes about two minutes.

## 02 — What UDUFood is

UDUFood is a Singapore app that turns surplus food into complementary meals.

| Role | What they do |
|---|---|
| **Restaurant** | You post leftover portions, display a QR, and hand over the food. |
| **Receiver** | Someone nearby browses listings and claims one complementary meal per day. |

**One-line leave-behind**

> UDUFood — list leftover portions as complementary meals. Nearby people claim with a QR. You stay in control. No fee.

## 03 — Full pitch (about 3 minutes)

### The problem (15 seconds)

Every day, good food is left over. Throwing it away costs you money and doesn’t help anyone. At the same time, people nearby need a proper meal.

### How it works for the restaurant (90 seconds)

**1. Sign up**  
Register with your phone number, restaurant details, and UEN. There is no approval wait — once registered, they go straight into the restaurant portal and can post immediately.

**2. Post a listing**  
From Home, tap Post. Enter dish name, category, quantity (packs, portions, boxes…), an optional photo, and a schedule: one-time, every day, or custom days.

**3. Display the QR**  
When you post, the app creates a QR code for that listing. Put it at the counter or print it. That is what the receiver scans.

**4. They claim, you hand over**  
A receiver nearby sees your listing (default 5 km; they can change this to 1, 2, 3, 5, or 10 km in settings), comes in, and scans the food QR. The meal is claimed and **automatically marked Collected** — you do not confirm or tap Collected. The app already checks they have not claimed today (limit: 1 per Singapore calendar day).

**5. Today’s claims board**  
Open Claims to see today’s pickups. Scanning the food QR already marks them Collected. If they don’t come, mark No-show and the portion returns to the pool.

**6. Close anytime**  
If you run out or want to stop, open the listing and tap Close. You can reactivate it later.

### Close the pitch (15 seconds)

You choose what to share, how much, and when. No extra staff workflow, no fee, no payment handling. Surplus food helps someone nearby instead of going in the bin.

## 04 — A typical day

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| **3:30 pm** | Kitchen has 8 portions of chicken rice left. Staff opens UDUFood, taps Post: “Chicken rice, 8 portions, today only.” QR goes on the counter. |
| **4:00–7:00 pm** | People nearby see it in the app, come in, scan, collect. Each person can claim once today. Staff glances at Claims if needed. |
| **7:30 pm** | 6 collected, 2 no-shows. Staff taps No-show on those two. If nothing is left, they Close the listing. |
| **Next day** | If it’s a regular leftover, they can set it to Every day so they don’t retype it. |

## 06 — If they push back

**Will this disrupt service?**  
No extra confirmation per customer. They scan the food QR, it is marked Collected automatically, you hand over. Same as giving a takeaway.

**Is there a fee?**  
No. Posting complementary meals is free for restaurant partners.

**Who is coming into my shop?**  
People with a UDUFood receiver account, nearby (default 5 km, or the radius they chose), one claim per day. Not an open public queue.

**What if they don’t show?**  
Mark No-show. That portion can be claimed again.

**What if I run out?**  
Close the listing. Nothing else is shown as available.

**Food safety / leftover quality?**  
You decide what to list and how much. List only food you would still serve.

**Do I need to confirm every claim?**  
No. Scanning the food QR marks it Collected automatically. You only mark no-show on the board if they don’t come.

**How long to go live?**  
Register today. There is no approval wait — once registered, they can open the portal and post straight away.
